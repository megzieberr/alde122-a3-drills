Referencing and Cohesion

ALDE122 semester test prep · 24 questions · 24 marks

Before you start

This drill covers the referencing and cohesion part of the semester test: naming a source from its fingerprint, picking the correct NWU Harvard entry, in-text citations, using sources fairly, and cohesion (a bare "This", connectors and transition sentences).

24 questions, 1 mark each, about 25 minutes. Every source in it is invented.

The most useful habit: Find the fingerprint first. Place: Publisher = book. volume(issue):pages = journal article. URL + Date of access = web page. In: + eds. = chapter.

Tap an answer and the explanation appears straight away. No timer. Italics in an entry are part of the answer, so look at them closely.
